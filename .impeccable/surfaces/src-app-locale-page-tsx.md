## Scope and mode

The whole corporate site, both locales: home (primary), divisions index, three division pages,
corporate, contact, two legal pages, 404, error. Visitor mode: Persuade (legal pages: Read).

Audience: counterparties vetting the company (suppliers, banks, prospective clients). Job: confirm
Veltrex is real and substantial, grasp the three-division structure, find the legal identity and
call. Proof available: legal name, Vergi No, address, two phones, the logo. Nothing else may be
invented. Copy latitude: short additions built only from what the site already says.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A holding company's facade, drawn rather than photographed. The site stands as a curtain
wall of hairline mullions at the Veltrex fold angle (59.036 degrees), alternating deep navy bands
with warm paper. It refuses the stock tower photo with three service cards, and the bare hairline
document the previous build shipped.

OWN-WORLD: Navy #082358 and void #050f28 bands; paper #f9f5ef bands; brand blue #0065EA only for
the mark, the primary action and live accents; ramp 300 for accents on navy. Inline SVG facade: two
tower planes of mullions and glass panes at 59 degrees, separated by a lit sky seam. Vertical
hairline rules split columns. Noto Serif Display light with italic accents; Albert Sans body.
Square corners, outlined ghost buttons on navy, solid blue primary.

STORY: The visitor sees a substantial İstanbul company, reads three divisions under one structure,
finds the registered identity (legal name, Vergi No, address) and calls a listed number.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 100svh navy facade band. Transparent 4.5rem header: paper mono logo left at 2.25rem,
nav with hairline separators right, centred on the header midline. Display headline left at up to
6rem, weight 400, in one colour. Along the foot, under a drawn hairline, the three divisions as ruled
columns (name and intro), static. Towers slide along 59 degrees into place, mullions draw, headline
rises; then lanes of panes on the far tower jump along floors and bays like current in a chip.
Towers drift on scroll.

Revised after design review: the two colour headline, the headline, intro and button stack, the
plaque strip, the sheen pass, the division "read more" links and the repeated divisions blocks were
all removed as template tells or repetition.

FORM: Curtain Wall, rank 1 on the ordered grounded list, chosen by the user as the pick card over
the assigned candidate 6 (The Fold); seed key 04899744.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
